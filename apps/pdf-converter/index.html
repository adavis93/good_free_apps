Conversion quality reference
📝→📄
Word → PDF Excellent
Converted via LibreOffice — the same engine that powers professional document workflows. Styles, tables, headers/footers, images, and page layout are reproduced with near-pixel-perfect accuracy.
📄→📝
PDF → Word High
Uses pdf2docx to reconstruct Word elements from PDF geometry. Text, bold/italic, tables, images, and approximate layouts are preserved. Complex multi-column or heavily styled PDFs may need minor adjustments.
📄→📋
PDF → Text Excellent
Extracts text via PyMuPDF, reading directly from the PDF's internal character data. Handles tracked-out display type, multi-column layouts, and font metadata with high accuracy.
📋→📄
Text → PDF High
Typesets your text using ReportLab with Times 11pt, 1-inch margins, and proper Unicode support. Produces clean, print-ready output. Source formatting is not inferred — what you type is what you get.
